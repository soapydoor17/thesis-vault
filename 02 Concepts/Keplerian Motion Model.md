## Definition
This describes orbital motion as the classical two-body problem: a smaller body moving under the gravitational pull of a much larger, spherically symmetric central body, with both treated as point masses. The resulting trajectories are ellipses, parabolas, or hyperbolas obeying Kepler's three laws, and the model deliberately ignores perturbing effects like third-body gravity, drag, and the central body's non-spherical shape (which is taken into account in [[Perturbed Circular Motion Model]]).
