## Definition
A TLE is a standardized data format that encodes an Earth-orbiting object's mean orbital elements into two 69-character lines of text, originally developed by NORAD for punched-card systems and largely unchanged for decades. It's built specifically to feed the SGP4/SDP4 propagation models used to predict a satellite's position and velocity from a given epoch.

Sometimes referred to as three line element with name on line 0

[[Keplerian Orbital Elements]] are on line 2 (ish)
- Inclination
- RAAN
- Eccentricity
- Arg. Peri
- Mean Anomaly
- Motion (revs/day)
	- Instead of semi-major axis
