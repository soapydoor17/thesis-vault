## Definition
Range rate is the time rate of change of slant range — essentially the target's radial velocity relative to the observer — and is measured directly via the Doppler shift of a returned signal, related to it by range rate = (wavelength × Doppler frequency)/2. It's why Doppler tracking systems can determine radial velocity even without directly measuring range itself
