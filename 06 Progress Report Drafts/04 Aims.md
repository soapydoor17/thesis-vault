#draft #progress-report 

> The Aims section should provide an overview of what you initially planned to carry out during the course of the semester. For example, your supervisor may have explicitly provided you with a set of goals to be achieved during the semester or else you may have written a plan of what you wished to achieve during the semester.
> 
> It is important that this section contains an honest and accurate account of what you had originally planned to carry out during the semester. You need to provide sufficient information so as to allow the reader to decide how well you have performed against an agreed plan. Of course, it may well be the case that you were not able to reach the agreed targets due to circumstances beyond your control – if this is the case then those reasons need to be outlined in the next section.
